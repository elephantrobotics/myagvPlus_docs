# 1 Installation Instructions

## 1.1 Installation of robotic arm

Firstly the arm needs to be mounted to the myAGV Plus. The arm can be mounted in the upper part of the myAGV Plus using LEGO keys or screws, either at the front or at the back depending on your needs.

<img src="../resources/7-ExamplesRobotsUsing/复合机器人/myAGV2023.png" alt="开启小车launch终端" style="zoom:67%;" />

## 1.2 Connecting the robot arm

Using the DC power cable to connect to the arm power supply connector and the other end to connect to the arm power connector, the trolley can supply power to the arm (12V 5A).

<img src="../resources/7-ExamplesRobotsUsing/复合机器人/myAGV2023API.jpg" alt="myAGV" style="zoom: 67%;" />

Example: mechArm 270, the same applies to other robotic arms.

1. Use a hex wrench to remove these four M4*8 hexagon socket screws

![开启小车launch终端](../resources/7-ExamplesRobotsUsing/复合机器人/A1.png)

2. Install the correct direction of the X-axis corresponding to the mechArm 270 robotic arm, and screw in the four M4*8 screws to secure the mechArm 270.
