# Examples Robots Using

The typical use case scenarios for myagv Plus often involve pairing with robotic arms to form compound robots, enabling mobile grasping, expanding workspace, and accomplishing a wider range of tasks. Below are the robotic arms supported by myagv Plus.

**myCobot 280 M5/Pi**

**myPalletizer 260 M5/Pi**

**mechArm 270 M5/Pi**

**myarm 300 pi**

**ultraArm P340**
