## Suitable User Groups

- **Teachers and Students in the Education Sector**: Given the potential of myAGV Plus in educational research, it is particularly suitable for STEM courses and robotics projects in secondary and higher education institutions. Teachers can use myAGV Plus to design practical courses, while students can use it to learn programming, robotics control theory, and basic knowledge of artificial intelligence.

- **Researchers**: Researchers can use myAGV Plus for prototype development and testing in experimental research on robotics, artificial intelligence, and automation. Its flexibility and scalability make it an ideal choice for research laboratories.

- **Business Demonstration and Marketing Professionals**: Companies can use myAGV Plus as a demonstration tool in exhibitions or events to effectively capture audience attention and showcase the company's technical strength and innovative products.

- **Hobbyists and Self-learners**: For individuals interested in robotics, regardless of their technical background, myAGV Plus provides a convenient and affordable platform to learn and explore programming, robotics, and artificial intelligence applications.
