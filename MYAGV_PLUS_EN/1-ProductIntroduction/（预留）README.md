## Product Introduction

myAGV Plus is a comprehensive upgrade of myAGV Jetson Nano 2023. The chassis is equipped with the **NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super core processor**, specifically designed to meet customers' high-performance computing needs in scenarios such as **image vision processing and AI algorithm deployment**. It features competition-grade Mecanum wheels paired with a fully enclosed metal frame, balancing mobility and structural durability. Based on the ROS2 development platform, it comes with three mainstream SLAM algorithms, fully meeting users' learning and development needs in mapping and navigation. It also offers rich expansion interfaces, supports the installation of my series robotic arms, enabling mobile grasping and other complex functions, helping users explore more application scenarios.
