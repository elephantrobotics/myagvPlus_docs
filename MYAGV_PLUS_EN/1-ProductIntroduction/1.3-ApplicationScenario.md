## Recommended Use Cases

- **Education**: Use myAGV Plus for programming teaching, robot design and manufacturing, and interdisciplinary science research projects to stimulate students' creativity and problem-solving skills.

- **Research**: Use myAGV Plus as a research tool in the fields of robotics, artificial intelligence, and automation to conduct algorithm testing, data collection, and model validation.

- **In Business Demonstrations**: Program myAGV Plus to perform engaging tasks or simulate real workflows to showcase technological innovation and enhance brand influence.

- **For Personal Learning and Entertainment**: Individual users can learn basic programming, robot control, and even advanced concepts in machine learning and artificial intelligence through myAGV Plus, as well as use it as an interesting personal project or hobby.
