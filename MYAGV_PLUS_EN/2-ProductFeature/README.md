# Product Feature

## Introduction

<img src="../resources/2-ProductFeature/2.1/JN-main.png " width="800" height="auto" />


myAGV Plus uses the NVIDIA® Jetson Orin Nano Super 8GB core motherboard, paired with the Ubuntu Mate 22.04 operating system specially customized by Elephant Robotics for robots, which is smooth and easy to use; myAGV Plus features multiple functions such as 2D mapping and navigation, 3D mapping and navigation, graphical programming, visual software, ROS simulation, and controller/keyboard control, making it the first choice for scientific research, education, and personal makers.

- **Scientific Research in Education**

    - The myAGV Plus is equipped with a high-performance FOC planetary brushless reduction motor, giving it stronger kinetic energy and enabling it to handle more complex application scenarios, while also significantly improving the accuracy of automatic navigation. From laboratory research to classroom teaching, this chassis provides comprehensive support, helping you explore the unknown and making scientific research and education more flexible.

- **Creative Development**

    - myAGV Plus is an upgrade based on myAGV Jetson Nano 2023, achieving perfect backward compatibility and retaining all the functions of myAGV. myAGV Plus is equipped with Jetson Orin Nano Super 8GB, providing strong image processing capabilities for the device, and also supports 3D mapping and navigation.

- **Business Exploration**

    - myAGV plus can be equipped with our company's 5 types of robotic arms. myAGV plus + robotic arm = infinite possibilities. With automatic navigation, machine vision, and end-effectors that can simulate various commercial scene applications, it can meet the application needs of complex projects. It supports composite robot kits, simulates warehouse logistics sorting scenarios, and can be used for commercial research and composite robot scenario demonstrations, among other things.
